01

About

This capstone project explored a service for Montgomery County employees. The experience was designed to help people form a useful request when a workplace challenge might be addressed with artificial intelligence.

The goal was not merely to collect a submission; it was to help the requester recognize, describe, and scope an opportunity in a way that supports later review.

02

Problem

How can we prompt users to create an accurate, appropriate request for a real problem on a smart, multifunctional form?

  • Employees may not know what a solvable AI opportunity looks like within their workflow.
  • Public-sector teams can struggle to identify tasks that could be performed more effectively with AI support.
  • People may have limited exposure to practical, day-to-day AI use cases.

03

Process

Journey mappingMapped the requester's steps, questions, and uncertainties before they submit an AI request.
Guided promptsTurned open-ended problem descriptions into clearer, more reviewable information.
Interface conceptsExplored a staged form flow with examples, contextual help, and a clear submission state.
Service touchpointsConsidered how the form connects with surrounding work tools and review processes.

04

Outcome

A guided request flow that makes the path from an observed work problem to a potential AI use case more legible.

The final design combines a structured form with prompts that encourage users to communicate the problem statement, context, and intended outcome—not simply request “AI.”
