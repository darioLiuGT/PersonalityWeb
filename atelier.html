My journey

Not an elite-by-default story. A self-built one.

This is the branding I stand behind: I know what it feels like to start from behind, rebuild, and climb anyway.

01. Early start

Bottom-ranked at a key public middle school in China

I did not begin as the obvious success story. Starting from the bottom taught me how shame, comparison, and low confidence can shape a student long before college applications begin.

02. York University

Psychology

I studied psychology at York University and built a foundation in how people think, behave, and make meaning before I formally entered HCI.

03. Xavor

Two years in UX research

I spent two years in UX research, learning how to turn ambiguity into evidence and evidence into product decisions.

04. UMD College Park

Human-Computer Interaction

At Maryland I found a clearer HCI direction and learned how to connect research questions, design methods, and long-form academic storytelling.

05. Gen.G

R&D and product experimentation

My work at Gen.G strengthened the link between research, product thinking, and execution under real constraints.

06. Georgia Tech

Human-Centered Computing Ph.D.

Georgia Tech is where the research, the advising, and the long-range vision now come together. It is also why I can guide students across both Master's and Ph.D. HCI applications with more depth.
